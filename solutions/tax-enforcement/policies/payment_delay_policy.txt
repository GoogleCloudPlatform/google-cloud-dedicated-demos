TAX PAYMENT DEADLINE AND LATE PAYMENT POLICY

Section 1: Standard Payment Deadlines

Tax payments are due according to the following schedule:
- Income tax: Due within 4 weeks of tax assessment notice
- Quarterly advance payments: Due on March 10, June 10, September 10, and December 10
- VAT payments: Monthly or quarterly depending on business size
- Business tax: Due in quarterly installments based on previous year assessment

Section 2: Late Payment Interest

Interest on late payments is calculated as follows:
- 0.5% per month (6% per annum) on unpaid amounts
- Interest begins accruing the day after the payment deadline
- Interest is compounded monthly
- No minimum interest threshold - interest applies to any unpaid amount
- Interest charges are non-negotiable and non-waivable

Section 3: Late Payment Penalties

In addition to interest, late payments may incur:
- Administrative penalty of 1% of unpaid tax for each month of delay
- Collection fees and enforcement costs
- Bank levy fees if direct seizure is required
- Legal fees if court proceedings are initiated
- Travel costs for in-person collection visits

Section 4: Severe Late Payment Violations

Payment delays exceeding 60 days are considered severe and trigger:
- Automatic enforcement proceedings
- Asset seizure authorization
- Bank account garnishment
- Wage garnishment for employed taxpayers
- Property liens and forced sales
- Travel restrictions for amounts over €10,000

Section 5: Payment Plans and Installments

Taxpayers experiencing genuine financial hardship may request payment plans:
- Maximum duration of 36 months for amounts under €50,000
- Extended plans up to 72 months for larger amounts with security
- Interest continues to accrue during payment plan period
- Monthly payments must be at least €100 or 10% of total, whichever is greater
- Missed installment payments result in immediate plan cancellation
- Collateral may be required for plans exceeding €25,000

Section 6: Eligibility for Payment Plans

Payment plan requests require:
- Detailed financial disclosure (income, assets, liabilities)
- Bank statements for previous 6 months
- Proof of inability to pay full amount
- Commitment to remain current on future tax obligations
- Agreement to automatic bank debit for installments

Payment plans are denied for taxpayers who:
- Have defaulted on previous payment plans
- Are under criminal tax investigation
- Have undisclosed assets or income
- Refuse to provide required financial documentation

Section 7: Business Taxpayer Payment Requirements

Business taxpayers face stricter payment enforcement:
- Late payment may result in business license suspension
- Directors become personally liable for unpaid corporate taxes
- Insolvency proceedings may be initiated for chronic non-payment
- Business bank accounts are priority targets for seizure
- VAT late payment affects cross-border trading privileges

Section 8: Repeat Late Payment Offenders

Chronic late payers (3+ late payments in 24 months) face:
- Mandatory security deposits for future tax obligations
- Required quarterly advance payments increased by 25%
- Loss of payment plan eligibility
- Automatic asset lien registration
- Enhanced audit scrutiny
- Reporting to credit bureaus

Section 9: Valid Reasons for Payment Delay

Extensions may be granted for legitimate reasons:
- Sudden unemployment with proof of job loss
- Serious illness or hospitalization with medical documentation
- Natural disaster or force majeure affecting assets
- Bank errors with documentation
- Identity theft or fraud affecting accounts

Extension requests must be submitted before payment deadline with supporting evidence.

Section 10: Voluntary Payment Incentives

Taxpayers who consistently pay on time or early receive:
- Reduced audit probability
- Favorable consideration for future payment plan requests
- Waiver of penalties for first-time late payment
- Simplified procedures for future tax matters
- Priority processing of tax refunds

Section 11: Enforcement Hierarchy

Tax authorities follow this enforcement progression:
1. Payment reminder notice (5 days after deadline)
2. Final demand letter (15 days after deadline)
3. Notice of enforcement action (30 days after deadline)
4. Bank account inquiry and garnishment (45 days)
5. Asset seizure and property liens (60 days)
6. Forced sale of assets and public auction (90 days)
7. Criminal proceedings for willful evasion (120+ days)

Section 12: International Taxpayers

Non-resident taxpayers with tax obligations:
- Must maintain local bank account for tax payments
- Face immediate enforcement without reminder notices
- May have travel restrictions imposed for unpaid taxes over €5,000
- Subject to international tax collection agreements
- Risk visa revocation and entry bans for chronic non-payment
