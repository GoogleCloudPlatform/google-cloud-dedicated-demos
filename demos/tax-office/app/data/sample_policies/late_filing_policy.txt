TAX FILING DEADLINE POLICY

Section 1: Standard Filing Deadlines

All taxpayers must file their annual tax declarations by July 31st of the following year. For example, tax year 2024 declarations must be filed by July 31st, 2025.

Section 2: Extended Deadlines for Tax Advisors

Taxpayers who engage certified tax advisors receive automatic extensions:
- February 28th of the second following year for standard cases
- Additional extensions may be granted upon request with valid justification

Section 3: Late Filing Penalties

Late filing penalties are calculated as follows:
- 0.25% of the assessed tax per month of delay
- Minimum penalty of €25 per month
- Maximum total penalty of 25% of the assessed tax
- Penalties apply even if no tax is owed

Section 4: Severe Delay Violations

Filing delays exceeding 90 days are considered severe violations and may trigger:
- Increased scrutiny of current and future tax declarations
- Mandatory audits for the late filing year
- Potential criminal investigation for tax evasion
- Suspension of business licenses for commercial taxpayers

Section 5: Repeat Offender Status

Taxpayers with consistent late filing patterns (3 or more late filings in 5 years) face:
- Automatic classification as high-risk taxpayers
- Annual mandatory audit requirements
- Loss of eligibility for simplified filing procedures
- Increased penalty rates (up to 50% higher)

Section 6: Valid Extensions

Extensions may be granted for legitimate reasons including:
- Serious illness with medical documentation
- Natural disasters or force majeure events
- Delayed receipt of essential tax documents from third parties
- Death in the immediate family

Extension requests must be submitted before the original deadline with supporting documentation.

Section 7: Electronic Filing Incentives

Taxpayers who consistently file electronically and on time for 5 consecutive years may qualify for:
- Reduced audit probability
- Simplified filing procedures
- Faster processing of tax refunds
- Waiver of late penalties for first-time violations within the 5-year period

Section 8: Business Taxpayer Requirements

Business taxpayers have additional obligations:
- Quarterly advance tax payments
- Monthly VAT declarations for businesses with turnover exceeding €600,000
- Annual business tax declarations separate from income tax
- Late filing of business declarations may result in business license suspension
